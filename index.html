ABOUT THE UNIT

Established in 2010, the NCC unit at NIT Arunachal Pradesh aims to groom students into disciplined and patriotic citizens. We participate in various National Integration Camps, Republic Day Parades, and Social Service activities.

🎯 MISSION

To develop character, comradeship, discipline, leadership, secular outlook and spirit of adventure & sportsmanship among our cadets.

🏆 VISION

To be the premier youth organisation of India producing future leaders who are motivated, disciplined and technically sound.

📅 ESTABLISHED

The NCC unit at NIT Arunachal Pradesh was established in 2010 and has since grown into a vibrant corps with representation at state and national levels.

🌏 ACTIVITIES

Republic Day Parade, National Integration Camps, Adventure Activities, Social Service, Disaster Management, and Community Development.
